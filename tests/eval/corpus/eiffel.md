# Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower located on the Champ de
Mars in Paris, France. It was designed by the engineer Gustave Eiffel and
built between 1887 and 1889 as the entrance arch for the 1889 World's Fair.

At 330 metres tall, the Eiffel Tower was the tallest man-made structure in
the world from 1889 until 1930, when the Chrysler Building in New York
surpassed it. It is painted with a special shade known as "Eiffel Tower
brown" and is repainted approximately every seven years.

The tower has three levels open to visitors, with restaurants on the first
and second levels. The top level's upper observation deck is the highest
observation platform accessible to the public in the European Union.
