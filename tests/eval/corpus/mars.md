# Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet in
the Solar System after Mercury. It is often called the "Red Planet" because
of the iron oxide on its surface that gives it a reddish appearance visible
from Earth with the naked eye.

Mars has two natural moons, Phobos and Deimos, which are small and
irregularly shaped. A day on Mars lasts approximately 24 hours and 37
minutes, very close to the length of an Earth day; a Martian year, however,
lasts about 687 Earth days.

The tallest known volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons, is on Mars and
rises about 22 kilometres above the surrounding plains. Mars has been the
target of many uncrewed missions, including the Curiosity rover that
landed on the planet in 2012.
