# Pyramids of Giza

The Pyramids of Giza are a complex of ancient Egyptian pyramids located on
the Giza Plateau, on the outskirts of Cairo, Egypt. The complex includes
three primary pyramids: the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the Pyramid of Khafre,
and the Pyramid of Menkaure.

The Great Pyramid of Khufu was built around 2560 BCE and stands roughly
146 metres tall, making it the tallest man-made structure in the world for
over 3,800 years. It was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and
the only one that still survives today.

The Great Sphinx of Giza, a limestone statue with the body of a lion and
the head of a pharaoh, lies on the same plateau and is widely believed to
have been carved during the reign of Khafre, the second pyramid's namesake.
